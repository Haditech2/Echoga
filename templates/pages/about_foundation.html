About ECHOGA Foundation

Empowering Communities Through Sustainable Development

Who We Are

ECHOGA Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering communities through sustainable development initiatives in education, healthcare, youth empowerment, and humanitarian services.

Founded by Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah, the foundation operates with a vision of creating lasting positive change in underserved communities across Nigeria.

We believe in a holistic approach to community development, addressing immediate needs while building long-term capacity for self-sufficiency and growth.

Explore Our Programs

Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah, Founder & Chairman

Our Mission

To empower communities through sustainable development initiatives that create lasting positive change in education, healthcare, and economic opportunities, fostering self-reliance and prosperity.

Our Vision

A thriving society where every individual has access to quality education, healthcare, and opportunities for personal and community growth, regardless of their socioeconomic background.

Our Values

Integrity, compassion, excellence, transparency, community participation, and sustainable impact guide all our initiatives and partnerships.

Our Core Focus Areas

Comprehensive initiatives for holistic community development

Education

Scholarships, learning materials, school infrastructure, and teacher training programs.

Youth Empowerment

Skills acquisition, mentorship, entrepreneurship support, and leadership development.

Healthcare

Medical outreach, health awareness, facility support, and preventive care programs.

Humanitarian Aid

Emergency relief, food security, shelter support, and disaster response.
