About the Founder

Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah - A Visionary Leader

Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah

Founder & Chairman, ECHOGA Foundation | Aspirant, Kogi State House of Assembly (Ankpa II)

Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah is a distinguished leader, philanthropist, and community development advocate dedicated to transforming lives and empowering communities across Nigeria.

With a deep commitment to social justice and sustainable development, Hon. Attah has dedicated his life to creating opportunities for the less privileged, particularly in the areas of education, healthcare, and youth empowerment.

His vision of a prosperous and equitable society has driven the establishment of ECHOGA Foundation, through which he channels his passion for community service and humanitarian work.

Leadership Philosophy

Guided by principles of service, integrity, and compassion

Servant Leadership

Believing that true leadership is about serving others and empowering communities to achieve their full potential.

Sustainable Development

Committed to creating lasting change through sustainable initiatives that address root causes of poverty and inequality.

Community-Centered Approach

Ensuring that all initiatives are driven by community needs and involve active participation of beneficiaries.

Innovation & Excellence

Embracing innovative solutions and maintaining the highest standards in all foundation activities.

Key Achievements

Milestones in community service and development

Community Leadership Award

Recognized for outstanding contributions to community development and social welfare initiatives.

Education Advocate

Provided scholarships and educational support to over 1,000 students from underprivileged backgrounds.

Healthcare Champion

Organized numerous medical outreach programs benefiting thousands in underserved communities.

Infrastructure Development

Led multiple community infrastructure projects including schools, healthcare centers, and water facilities.

"My vision is to create a society where every individual, regardless of their background, has access to quality education, healthcare, and opportunities to thrive. Through ECHOGA Foundation, we are not just providing aid; we are building sustainable pathways to prosperity and empowerment."

Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah
Kogi State House of Assembly Aspirant (Ankpa II)
