Meet Our Founder

Visionary Leadership Driving Community Transformation

Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah

Founder & Chairman, ECHOGA Foundation

Aspirant, Kogi State House of Assembly (Ankpa II)

Hon. Ismaila Muhammed Attah, popularly known as Echoga, is a distinguished leader, philanthropist, and community development advocate dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria.

Currently a prominent leader and community advocate, Hon. Attah is aspiring to represent the good people of Ankpa II Constituency at the Kogi State House of Assembly, combining his political leadership with philanthropic work to create meaningful change.

With unwavering commitment to social justice and sustainable development, Hon. Attah has dedicated his life to creating opportunities for the less privileged through education, healthcare, youth empowerment, and community development initiatives.

Key Achievements

Milestones in Hon. Attah's Journey of Service

Education Transformation

Spearheaded the renovation of 5 community schools and provided full scholarships to over 200 indigent students, ensuring quality education for the next generation.

Healthcare Access

Facilitated free medical outreach programs reaching 5,000+ residents and donated critical medical equipment to 3 local health centers.

Clean Water Initiative

Installed 10 solar-powered boreholes in rural communities, providing access to clean and safe drinking water for over 2,000 households.
